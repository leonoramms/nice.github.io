# NICE and particle physics at the DFS Annual Meeting 2026 **8–9 June 2026** NICE researchers joined physicists from across Denmark for the Danish Physical Society’s Annual Meeting at the University of Southern Denmark in Odense. The NICE session, chaired by Ian Bearden, also served as NICE’s annual national meeting and provided a forum for presenting current CERN-related research and discussing future activities. The NICE session highlighted several areas of experimental particle and nuclear physics. Leonora Misciattelli Mocenigo Soranzo presented simulation studies for tau physics at the proposed Future Circular Collider, Zhiyong Lu discussed nuclear-structure studies using ultra-relativistic collisions in ALICE, and Thomas Schiøler presented the new ATLAS Inner Tracker being developed for the High-Luminosity LHC. Particle physics continued on the second day in the Subatomic Physics session. Talks by Maxim Virta, Martijn Laarhoven and Daniel Murnane covered Bayesian methods for understanding nuclear structure, collective flow in small collision systems at the LHC, and developments in machine learning for ATLAS and the High-Luminosity LHC. NICE-associated researchers and students were also strongly represented in the poster session. Among the meeting’s 34 posters, NICE participants received awards in both the senior and junior categories—an excellent recognition of the quality of the research and the enthusiasm with which it was presented. The meeting demonstrated the breadth of Danish particle-physics activities and the important role of NICE in connecting researchers, students and CERN-based experiments across Denmark.
