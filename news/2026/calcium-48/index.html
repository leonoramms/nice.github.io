New publication explores the neutron skin of calcium-48

A new open-access article investigates how collisions at CERN’s Large Hadron Collider could be used to study the neutron skin of calcium-48.

The neutron skin describes the difference between the distributions of neutrons and protons inside an atomic nucleus. In the new study, researchers use the AMPT simulation model to compare collisions of calcium-40 nuclei with collisions of calcium-48 nuclei.

Collective flow at the LHC

The study examines whether the thickness of the neutron skin in calcium-48 leaves a measurable imprint on the collective motion of particles produced in high-energy nuclear collisions.

The simulations indicate that several flow observables and measures of transverse-momentum fluctuations are sensitive to changes in the neutron-skin thickness.

Publication

Vitsos, A., Soranzo, L. M. M., Nielsen, E. G. D. et al.

“Characterizing the neutron skin of ⁴⁸Ca through collective flow at the CERN Large Hadron Collider.”

European Physical Journal C, volume 86, article 536, 2026.
